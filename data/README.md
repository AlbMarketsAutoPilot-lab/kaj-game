# Open data

`countries.json` is a small extract (the 174 countries on the 30-turn map: names, capitals,
currencies, land borders, area) of [mledoze/countries](https://github.com/mledoze/countries),
made by `node scripts/challenges-make.ts --from <countries.json>`.

It is used under the **Open Database License (ODbL 1.0)** (`LICENSE-ODbL.md`). The extract and
the challenge questions made from it (`src/challenges/challenges.ts`) are shared under the same
licence. Credit shown in the app: "Country data: mledoze/countries, ODbL 1.0".

Flags (`assets/flags/`) are from [flag-icons](https://github.com/lipis/flag-icons) by
Panayiotis Lipiridis, MIT licence (`assets/flags/LICENSE`).
