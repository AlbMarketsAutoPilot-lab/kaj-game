# KAJ v1 — scope and decisions

Status: **approved by the owner**. This file sets what v1 contains.
Where it differs from the rulebook (`KAJ-rules-v7.md`), **this file wins for v1**.
Target: a complete, playable v1 within about 15 days of work.

---

## 1. Platform

- **Android app only.** A WebView app built in Android Studio. The game files (HTML, JS, SVG, sounds) are **packed inside the APK**, so the game works fully offline.
- The game itself is written with web technology (TypeScript).
- **Cloudflare is optional:** only a free preview link during development. The game does not need a server.
- **GitHub Actions** builds the signed APK.
- Play Store submission is out of scope (handled separately by the owner).
- English only. Completely free, no ads, no accounts, no chat.

## 2. Players

- **2–4 seats on one device.** Each seat is a human or a robot. At least 1 human, at most 3 robots.
- **Robots: three levels, easy, normal and hard** (owner's change, task 13). One robot with a level setting, chosen for each robot seat on the setup screen (default normal). Easy answers quizzes, the exam and challenges right about 50% of the time, normal about 75%, hard about 90%.
- **Turn order:** random first player, then always the same order.
- **Nothing is secret** (no keep cards), so there is no "pass the phone" screen. Exam answers are on the honour system.

## 3. Profiles

- The 4 profiles of the rulebook: Backpacker, Business Traveler, Luxury Traveler, Digital Nomad.
- **Each profile can be taken by only one player.** Profiles are chosen in turn order; the last player gets the one left.
- Board icons: 🎒 backpack (Backpacker), 💼 business suitcase (Business), 💎 diamond (Luxury), 💻 laptop (Nomad), plus each player's colour.

## 4. Map

- **Only the 30-turn map:** 53 areas (50 until task 14a, 52 until task 14b), 7 wonders, **8 airports** and **9 ports** with **13 two-way connections**. (Task 14b, owner-approved: ships Australia East ↔ New Zealand and USA West ↔ Alaska.) (Task 9b, owner-approved: UK & Ireland gets an airport to the Arabian Peninsula and Japan a port to USA West, so UK & Ireland and Japan have both.) An airport or port has **1 to 3 fixed destinations**; the traveller chooses one when boarding. Luxury can choose any airport or port. (Changed in task 2b, owner-approved: 6 airports + 4 ports with one destination each could not pass the map check.)
- **Starting continents: 6 choices**, all different between players: Europe, Asia, Africa, **North America**, **South America**, Oceania.
- **Welcome bonus:** Europe, Asia, Africa +3 · North America +4 · South America +4 · Oceania +5.
- Big countries: a country split into N parts gives +N when all parts are visited (e.g. USA East + USA West = +2).
- **Canada and Russia in 3 parts** (owner's change, task 14a): Canada West, Canada Central, Canada East; Russia West, Siberia, Russia Far East. Completing either gives **+5** (instead of +1 +3); the other big countries keep +1 +2 = +3.
- Map check (automatic): every island is reachable, and no player can ever be stuck forever, including behind a visa area.

## 5. Rules with 2–4 players

- All rulebook rules apply to every player: two players are never in the same area, and an area someone is travelling to by plane or ship is **booked** (nobody else may go there until they land; task 13); visas, tour fees and tickets go to **the player who owns** that citizenship or business; the first player to reach a wonder gets the right to buy its tours.
- Everything else follows `KAJ-rules-v7.md`, except the changes in section 9.

## 6. Quizzes and challenges

- **Travel-turn challenges** (the player may say yes or no; since task 12 there are no event cards on trips, and no challenge with 0 points): win +1, lose −1. Six types, all generated by script from open datasets (no drawings):

  | Challenge | Example |
  |---|---|
  | Which flag? | Flag shown → pick the country |
  | Which is bigger? | Two countries → pick the bigger one |
  | Which capital? | "Capital of Peru?" Lima / Quito |
  | Which continent? | "Where is Mali?" Africa / Asia |
  | Neighbours | "Which one borders France?" Spain / Poland |
  | Which currency? | "Japan pays with…" Yen / Won |

- **Airline quiz** (free ticket): one **text** a/b question about the destination (no drawings in v1).
- **Citizenship exam:** read 6 facts, then 3 a/b questions about them, in the same turn; **2 of 3 right to pass**, fewer: no citizenship (owner's change, task 14f).

### Timers (offline)

| What | Time limit | If time runs out |
|---|---|---|
| Challenges and airline quiz | 15 s | Wrong answer |
| Exam: reading 6 facts | **No timer** (owner's change, task 14f): "I'm ready" | — |
| Exam: each of the 3 questions | 15 s, started by the player ("Start") | Unanswered = wrong |
| Moves and decisions | **No timer** | — |

## 7. Content

- **Facts: about 12 per area** (enough for 2 different exams), drafted by AI and **fact-checked by the owner**.
- Capitals, currencies, borders, flags and sizes come from **open datasets by script**. Claude writes only the interesting facts.
- **Art:** SVG/HTML in the style of the owner's opening poster and background images (landscape and portrait, supplied by the owner).
- **Sound and music:** supplied by the owner; Claude may adjust or replace them.
- **Automatic save and resume.**

## 8. Engine design

- One rules engine with no screens, built around two functions:
  - `legalActions(state)`: every move allowed right now;
  - `apply(state, action)`: the new state after a move.
- A **random robot** (always picks a random legal move) plays thousands of games to check automatically:
  - nobody is ever stuck;
  - points never go below 0;
  - two players are never in the same area.
- The random robot is the base of the robot (random robot + simple strategy rules, three levels).
- The rulebook becomes automatic tests.
