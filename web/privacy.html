Kris Ann's Journey: Privacy Policy

Effective date: 10 October 2026

Kris Ann's Journey is a geography board game for children and families. It is available as an Android app and as a game on this website. This policy explains what happens to information when you play.

The short version

We do not collect any personal information. There are no accounts, no ads, no in-app purchases, no analytics and no tracking. The game does not ask for a name, an email address, a location, photos, contacts or any other personal information.

What the game keeps on your device

To let you continue a game later, the game saves your current game and a few settings (for example whether sounds are on) on your own device. This information never leaves your device through the game, and we cannot see it. Uninstalling the app, or clearing the website's data in your browser, deletes it.

On Android, your phone's own backup (for example to your Google account) may include these saved games, if backup is turned on. That backup is controlled by you and by your phone's settings, not by us.

The Android app

The app works fully offline. It does not have permission to use the internet and asks for no other permissions. It contains no ads and no third-party tools that collect data.

This website

The website is hosted by GitHub Pages. Like most web hosts, GitHub may record technical information such as IP addresses for security and to run its service. We do not receive or use this information. See the GitHub General Privacy Statement for details.

Children

The game is made for children, including children under 13. Because we collect no personal information, we collect none from children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new effective date.
